# Technical Debt, Areas of Concern & Known Gaps

**Analysis Date:** 2026-09-17

## Overview

While the MVP Milestone (M0–M6) and Workbench UI (Phases 8–9) are 100% verified with 235 passing tests, several architectural improvement areas and future milestones are documented below.

---

## 1. Physical Rules Maturity & Certification (MDA-002 Gap)

- **Status:** All physical rules in `MockSIA` and `PhysicsRuleMatrix` currently operate under the **`HYPOTHESIS`** status according to MDA v2.2 doctrine.
- **Concern:** Only `MDE_SAFETY_BROACH_001` has completed Stations 1–4 of the MDA-002 standard. Stations 5–10 (Monte Carlo 10,000+ fuzzing, fault injection, and Sea Trial validation) remain in the roadmap for production flight readiness.
- **Risk Mitigation:** Strict AST boundary tests prevent unvalidated heuristics from leaking into safety-critical firmware.

---

## 2. Real-Time Performance & Wall-Clock Speedup

- **Current State:** Pure Python 3.12 + NumPy/SciPy execution easily achieves >10x faster-than-real-time for single 100 Hz scenarios.
- **Future Scale:** For large Monte Carlo batch runs (10,000+ runs for MDA-002 Station 6), consider:
  - Vectorized multiprocessing across CPU cores;
  - Optional Numba / C-extension compilation of the RK4 physics step if scaling to 100,000+ runs.

---

## 3. Sensor Latency & Channel Classification

- **Status:** `safety_channel_status` (`WIRED_VERIFIED`, `WIRELESS_ADVISORY`, `MIXED`) is formally modeled in `SensorFrame`.
- **Observation:** Wireless 802.15.4 IMU configurations introduce 100–250 ms event latency. Safety guarantees (`<20 ms` hazard detection) require RS-485 wired sensors until wireless channels pass the Transient Test Suite.

---

## 4. Front-End Zero-Build Lifecycle

- **Architecture:** The Workbench UI uses native browser ES Modules without Webpack/Vite bundlers.
- **Benefit:** Zero build step, instant hot reload, lightweight maintenance.
- **Note:** Keep JavaScript modularized across `state.js`, `playback.js`, `modals.js`, and `app.js` to prevent monolithic growth as more dials are added.
