<objective>
Implement exhaustive unit and determinism verification tests for the physics engine:
1. Bit-for-bit repeatability across independent runs with identical scenario seeds.
2. Physical stability and bounded energy properties (stable upright equilibrium, roll damping).
3. Broach precursor dynamics replication (verifying the SIM-005 sequence: wave impact + wind gust → heel buildup → rudder stall → weather helm round-up).

Depends on Plans 03-01 and 03-02 being complete.
</objective>

<tasks>

## Task 1: Implement Determinism Verification Tests in `test_physics_determinism.py`

Create `tests/unit/test_physics_determinism.py`:
- 1000-step (10.0 s) simulation runs with identical initial conditions and seed produce 100% bit-identical trajectory states ($x, y, \psi, u, v, r, \phi, p$).
- Runs with different master seeds produce distinct environmental and vessel trajectories.
- Resetting `WorldModel` and `VesselDynamics` restores exact initial state.

## Task 2: Implement Physical Stability & Broach Precursor Tests in `test_broach_dynamics.py`

Create `tests/unit/test_broach_dynamics.py`:
- **Righting & Damping Invariant:** When released from initial 20° heel in zero wind/wave, vessel rolls back to 0° upright equilibrium with decayed oscillations.
- **Speed Invariant:** Vessel speed under steady 20 kt wind reaches realistic terminal velocity (5-8 kt for classic monohull).
- **SIM-005 Broach Precursor Sequence:**
  - Simulate SIM-005 scenario conditions (IOR monohull, TWS=20 kt, TWA=65°, Wave=3m/7s, Initial heel=15°).
  - Apply wave impact at T=10s and wind gust at T=12s.
  - Verify heel increases past 25° at T=12-14s.
  - Verify rudder authority drops and yaw rate turns sharply into the wind (weather helm round-up) at T=14-15s.
  - Verify `GroundTruthFrame` generation records this physical broach precursor state at 100 Hz.
</tasks>
