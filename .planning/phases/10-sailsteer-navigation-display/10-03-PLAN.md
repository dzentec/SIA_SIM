# Plan 10-03: Live & Playback Telemetry Integration & Validation against 6 Test Scenarios

## Objective
Wire real-time simulation ticks and playback scrubbing into `SailSteerController` and `SailSteerRenderer` in `playback.js`, and validate behavior against all 6 calibrated test scenarios defined in `SailSteer_BG.md` §7.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Connect `playback.js` to `SailSteerRenderer`
- In `renderAtTime(simTimeMs)`:
  - Extract sensor readings (`awa`, `aws`, `gpsSog`, `gpsCog`, `imuRoll`, `imuPitch`, `imuYaw/hdg`) and environment state (`tws`, `twd`, `currentSpeed`, `currentDir`, `waveElev`).
  - Assemble `SailSteerTelemetry` payload.
  - Call `SailSteerRenderer.render(telemetry, activeTick)`.
- In `renderZeroState()`:
  - Call `SailSteerRenderer.renderZeroState()` to set docked / standby displays.

### Task 2: Validate Against 6 Calibrated Test Scenarios from `SailSteer_BG.md` §7
1. **Scenario 1 (Normal Sailing — Close-Hauled, Port Tack):**
   - $HDG=155^\circ, STW=2.8\text{kn}, SOG=4.5\text{kn}, TWA=-57^\circ, AWA=-50^\circ, TWS=16.2\text{kn}, TWD=096^\circ, WPT\_BRG=107^\circ$.
   - Verify: $T$ needle at $-57^\circ$, $A$ needle at $-50^\circ$, Port upwind sector red, Starboard upwind sector green, WPT marker at $312^\circ$ relative, current vector at $+90^\circ$ length $57.5\text{px}$.
2. **Scenario 2 (Downwind Running, Starboard Tack):**
   - $HDG=030^\circ, TWA=+150^\circ, TWS=12.0\text{kn}, TWA_{\text{opt\_down}}=140^\circ$.
   - Verify: downwind sectors positioned at stern, $T$ needle at $+150^\circ$.
3. **Scenario 3 (Calm / Drifting):**
   - $TWS=0.2\text{kn}, TWA=\text{null}, AWA=\text{null}$.
   - Verify: tack sectors hidden, needles faded with low opacity, digital readouts show `---`.
4. **Scenario 4 (GPS Loss / Fix Lost):**
   - $gps\_lock=\text{false}, SOG=\text{null}, COG=\text{null}, POS=\text{null}$.
   - Verify: $SOG/COG/POS$ show `---`, GPS status indicator shows red/warning, WPT marker hidden.
5. **Scenario 5 (No Active Waypoint):**
   - $waypoint=\text{null}$.
   - Verify: $WPT\_DIST/BRG/ETW/STEUERN$ show `---`, name displays `NO WPT`.
6. **Scenario 6 (Head-to-Wind $TWA=0^\circ$):**
   - $TWA=0^\circ, AWA=0^\circ, TWS=10.0\text{kn}$.
   - Verify: $T$ needle directly over bow, upwind sectors highlighted.
